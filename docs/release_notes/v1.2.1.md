Fixes the cinematic mode's shaking.

**To install:** download `YSFlight-Replay-Viewer-v1.2.1-win64.zip` below, unzip it into a new folder, copy your `events` folder across, and double-click `YSFlight Replay Viewer.exe`.

- **The camera only shakes in the Flyby shot now,** as the aircraft rushes past and when something explodes nearby. Chase, Wingman, Ground camera, Orbit, Lock-on, Weapon, Crane and Drone keep still. The View tab's slider is now called **Flyby shake**. Zooming in doesn't make the shake bigger on screen.
- **Why it shook:** your game records other players' aircraft a little unevenly. Between network updates it moves them itself, then snaps them a few metres when the next update arrives. The shake read that unevenness as the aircraft rushing past the camera, so it never stopped. A zoomed-in lens also showed the unevenness itself, which is why the ground camera was the worst.
- **Smoother aircraft in cinematic mode:** every flight path is now smoothed over about a fifth of a second, so aircraft glide without the network wobble, even at full zoom. The scoring view still shows the replay's positions exactly as recorded.
