More cameras for the cinematic mode, a flight-stick mouse, crane helpers, and steadier Flyby and Ground cameras.

**To install:** download `YSFlight-Replay-Viewer-v1.3-win64.zip` below, unzip it into a new folder, copy your `events` folder across, and double-click `YSFlight Replay Viewer.exe`. Old events open as before.

- **More chase cameras (press 1, then 1 again to step through them; Shift+1 goes back):**
  - **Chase**: as before.
  - **Chase plane**: a camera plane flying formation off the aircraft's tail. In hard turns it can't keep up (it pulls at most 6 G), so it swings wide, catches up again, and banks as it turns.
  - **Trailing**: flies the aircraft's own path a little behind it. The wheel sets how far back.
  - **Delayed**: YSFlight's outside view with camera delay. The camera follows the aircraft as it was 0.8 s before, so the aircraft swings round in the picture when it turns.
  - **Outside**: YSFlight's F7 view. The camera looks from a direction fixed in the world, whatever the aircraft does.
- **Ghost cameras (new key 0; press 0 again for the next spot):** a camera bolted to the aircraft that turns with it. Spots: behind, wing, front, top, belly, tail, nose. The wheel moves it nearer or further out.
- **Mouse as a flight stick (F7, or the View tab):** the mouse is hidden and works like a stick. Push it a little off centre and the camera turns slowly; push further and it turns faster, easing in and out, like YSFlight 2012's F7 / F8 view. **F8** or the **middle mouse button** puts the stick back in the centre. The View tab sets the **Stick speed** and can flip up / down.
- **Crane helpers (8):** while the replay is paused, the path shows on screen: numbered points, a dashed line for the glide, and a short line for where the camera looks at each point. A line of keys at the top shows what to press:
  - **K**: add a point here
  - **U**: remove the last point
  - **Delete**: remove all points
  - **[ ]**: shorter / longer move
  - **V**: ready-made moves round the aircraft (sweep, rise, push in, pull out, circle)
  - **G**: hide or show the helpers

  They disappear while the move plays, so recordings stay clean.
- **Shot names:** when you step through the chase or ghost cameras, the camera's name shows for a moment at the top. **Before recording, press G** to hide all helpers (the names and the crane path).
- **Steadier Flyby (3) and Ground camera (4):**
  - These still cameras now follow a smoother version of the flight path.
  - They turn like a camera on a tripod, with no tilt.
  - Playing backwards no longer makes the flyby jump around.
  - The deck camera only rides a ship when it is placed on or next to that ship.
- **F9 saves a flight path:** if an aircraft still looks shaky, follow it and press F9 (in either mode). A small `.txt` file with its flight path from 20 s before to 20 s after is saved next to the event file. Send it over and the smoothing can be tuned to your replays.
- The hint line at the bottom of the screen no longer runs under the side panel.
