A calm camera for watching, a smoothing switch, and the top view in the cinematic mode.

**To install:** download `YSFlight-Replay-Viewer-v1.4-win64.zip` below, unzip it into a new folder, copy your `events` folder across, and double-click `YSFlight Replay Viewer.exe`.

- **Orbit (5) no longer turns by itself.** It works like the normal viewer's camera. It stays looking from the same direction, even when the aircraft turns, and only moves when you turn it (right-drag, or the stick with F7). The mouse wheel sets how far away it is.
- **The cinematic mode now starts in Orbit,** from where your camera already was. Use it to watch calmly, Tab between aircraft, and find where the next good shot will come from. Pressing 5 in any other shot also starts the orbit from where that camera is.
- **B: smooth camera on / off** (also a switch in the View tab). On, the cameras ease after the aircraft, as before. Off, they stick to it exactly, with no lag.
- **T: the top view works in the cinematic mode too.** It works as outside the mode: the wheel zooms, right-drag moves, and a click follows an aircraft. Press T again, or any shot key, to go back to 3D.
- The View tab's "Orbit speed" slider is gone. For a slow automatic circle, turn on the stick (F7) and hold the mouse a little to one side.
