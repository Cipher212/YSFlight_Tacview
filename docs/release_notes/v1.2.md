Cinematic mode, for making videos of RvB events.

**To install:** download `YSFlight-Replay-Viewer-v1.2-win64.zip` below, unzip it into a new folder, and double-click `YSFlight Replay Viewer.exe`. The window title shows the version. Your settings and events carry over if you copy the `events` folder across.

- **Cinematic mode:** press **M** (or the **Cinematic** button). Everything but the world is hidden: panels, name tags, lines, markers and the mouse pointer. Record with OBS. **M** or **Esc** brings it all back, **F1** lists its keys, **F11** is full screen.
- **Nine shots on keys 1-9:** Chase, Wingman, Flyby, Ground camera (long-lens zoom), Orbit, Weapon (rides a missile or bomb), Lock-on (over the shoulder), Crane (glides through points set with K) and Drone (free camera with weight).
- **Lens and time:** mouse wheel for distance, Ctrl+wheel to zoom, Alt+wheel for background blur. Hold Z for a snap zoom and X for smooth slow motion. Space eases to a pause, and orbit, crane and drone keep moving. Backspace retakes the shot from where you pressed Play.
- **Camera shake** comes from G-force, aircraft rushing past and nearby explosions. The View tab sets how strong it is.
- **Effects, YSFlight's style done better:** white missile smoke that billows and drifts, and flares that start red. Explosions go flash, fireball, sparks, then rising smoke; on the ground you also get dust, over water a splash. Aircraft going down burn and trail black smoke, and a wreck on the ground keeps smoking. Everything follows the replay's clock, so it slows down, freezes and runs backwards with it.
- **Every key can be changed:** View tab > **Keys...**, click a key, press the new one.
- The scoring views are unchanged. Leaving cinematic mode puts everything back as it was.
