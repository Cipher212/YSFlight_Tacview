Shows who sent replays.

**To install:** download `YSFlight-Replay-Viewer-v1.4.1-win64.zip` below, unzip it into a new folder, copy your `events` folder across, and double-click `YSFlight Replay Viewer.exe`.

- **The Files tab now starts with a summary of the replays:**
  - **Whole event:** players whose replays cover the whole event.
  - **Part of the event only:** players whose replays miss part of it, with what is missing, for example "starts at 12:03", "missing 30:00-31:20" or "ends at 1:20:00". A player who disconnected and sent two files counts as one player, and their files are joined together. Short breaks under 30 seconds don't count.
  - **Flew, but no replay used:** pilots who flew but whose replay wasn't used (never sent, or left out as another match).
- It works for old event files too, no rebuild needed.
